Specialized ENT & Hearing Care in Kampala

ORLA Specialized ENT Clinic focuses on the diagnosis and management of ear, nose and throat conditions, as well as audiology and hearing aid services. We are located on the Ground Floor of Kema Heights Building, adjacent to Texol Fuel Station at Nakulabye Traffic Lights.

Our Services

Specialized care for ear, nose, throat and hearing health.

Ear Conditions

Nose & Sinus Care

Throat Care
