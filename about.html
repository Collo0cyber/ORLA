About ORLA Specialized ENT Clinic

Specialized Care for Your Ear, Nose & Throat Health

ORLA Specialized ENT Clinic is a dedicated Ear, Nose and Throat clinic located in Kampala. We focus on the diagnosis and management of ENT conditions, as well as audiology and hearing aid services.

Our clinic is conveniently located on the Ground Floor of Kema Heights Building, adjacent to Texol Fuel Station at Nakulabye Traffic Lights, making it easy for patients to find us.

ENT Care

Assessment and management of ear, nose and throat conditions for adults and children.

Audiology Services

Professional hearing assessments and hearing care support.

Hearing Aids

Hearing aid services including fitting, support and repair.

Patient-Centered Care

We aim to provide clear communication and attentive care for every patient.
