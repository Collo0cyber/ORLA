5 Signs You Should See an ENT Specialist

Many ear, nose and throat problems are easy to dismiss as "just a cold" or "just tiredness" — until they become chronic. Here's when it's worth booking a consultation.

Ear, nose and throat conditions often start small: an ear that feels blocked, a sinus that never quite clears, a voice that stays hoarse a little too long. Because these symptoms can overlap with everyday colds and allergies, many people put off seeing a specialist until the problem has been going on for weeks or months. At ORLA Specialized ENT Clinic, we regularly see patients who wish they had come in sooner, simply because they weren't sure whether their symptoms warranted a visit.

If a symptom lasts longer than two to three weeks, or keeps coming back, it's worth having it checked rather than waiting it out.

Here are five signs that it's time to book an appointment with an ENT specialist:

1. Persistent ear pain or a feeling of fullness — especially if it's affecting your hearing or comes with discharge.

2. Chronic sinus congestion or facial pressure — nasal blockage or sinus pain that doesn't improve after a couple of weeks.

3. Hoarseness or voice changes lasting more than two weeks — particularly without an obvious cause like a cold.

4. Frequent or severe sore throats — recurring throat infections or difficulty swallowing.

5. Noticeable changes in hearing — including ringing in the ears (tinnitus) or difficulty following conversations.

If any of these sound familiar, our team can carry out a proper assessment and recommend the right next step, whether that's treatment, further tests, or a hearing evaluation.
